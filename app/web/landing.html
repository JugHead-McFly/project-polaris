One useful answer, before you set up

Polaris helps you decide what tonight looks like.

01

Read the night

It considers your observing location, darkness, Moon position, target visibility, and forecast at the time you would actually image.

02

Explain the recommendation

See a clear Proceed, Use Caution, or Do Not Image recommendation—plus the reason in normal language.

03

Keep you in control

Polaris is advisory only. It does not connect to, operate, or make safety decisions for your telescope.

Who the private alpha is for

For smart-telescope users who would rather observe than overthink.

  • You use a smart telescope such as DWARF, Seestar, or Vespera.
  • You want help making sense of weather, Moon conditions, targets, and timing.
  • You are comfortable trying unfinished software and telling us plainly what is useful—or confusing.

What to expect

A useful companion, still being built in the open.

Polaris is in private alpha. Recommendations may be unavailable when source data is unavailable, and they should always be checked against what you see at your site.

Your feedback directly shapes what we improve next. That is the whole point of this early stage.
