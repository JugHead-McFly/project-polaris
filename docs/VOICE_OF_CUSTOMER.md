## Interpretation rules

- Preserve screenshots or a source reference when the user has authorized
  sharing it with Polaris.
- Distinguish a stated feature request from the underlying job or frustration.
- Classify the proposed response as Must Build, Should Build, Nice to Have,
  Don't Build, or Parking Lot only after enough context is available.
- Record uncertainty rather than treating one loud comment as a market truth.
- Log voluntary statements of willingness to pay or test, but do not press
  participants for payment during early discovery.

[CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_SPRINT.md](CUSTOMER_DISCOVERY_SPRINT.md) for the research
